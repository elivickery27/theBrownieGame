Case #001 - The Stolen Brownie Recipe

Home Suspects Evidence Timeline Solve the Case

Amy Evans

Age:16

Role:Student/Kitchen Volunteer

Background

Amy is a Year 11 student at Westbrook Academy who volunteers in the school kitchen twice a week. She has helped perpare the famous brownies on several occasions and her help is appreciated as she is very enthusiasctic and careful in the kitchen.

Connection to the Recipe

Amy knows that the original brownie recipe is considered confidential and is normally kept inside the kitchen office. She has only glanced at it a few times.

Statement

"I went into the office to find some labels. I didn't touch anything other than the labels."

Alibi

  • 4:02 PM - Entered the kitchen office.
  • 4:07 PM - Seen washing mixing bowls in the sink.

Evidence Against Amy Evans

  • She was inside the office when the recipe could have disappeared.
  • She was carrying around a blue notebook.
  • She had previously asked about putting the recie online to make it easier to access for the staff.

Evidence in the Favour of Amy Evans

  • She doesn't normally have access to the orginal recipe.
  • there is no clear motive for stealing it
