RECIPE: Chocolate Brownies

RECIPE ID: WK-BR-2006

VERSION: 3.1

DATE CREATED: 14 October 2006

LAST UPDATED: 03 September 2024

200g Dark Chocolate

175g Unsalted Butter

250g Caster Sugar

3 Large Eggs

100g Plain Flour

30g Cocoa Powder

1 tsp Vanilla Extract

1 pinch of Salt

1. Preheat the oven to 180℃.

2. Melt the chocolate and Butter together and allow the mixture to cool slightly.

3. Mix the Sugar and Eggs together until fully combined.

4. Slowly add the melted Chocolate and Butter into the Eggs and Sugar mix.

5. Sift the Flour and Cocoa Powder into the mixture.

6.Add the Vanilla extract and Salt.

7. Pour the mixture into a lined baking tray.

8. Bake until the center is set but still slightly soft.

9. Allow to cool before cutting into squares.
