Evidence #002

The Paintbrush

Status:Active Evidence

Evidence Type:Physical/Trace Evidence

Location Found:Bushes approximatley 4 meters east of the statue.

Date Found:4 September 2025

Time Found:8:03 AM

Description

A small flat-ended paintbrush was discovered partially concealed beneath a bush near the eastern side of the courtyard. Blue paint residue was viible on the bristles.

Initial Observations

The brush appears to have been used recently

A partial fingerprint was recovered from the wooden handle.

Connection to the Case

The blue paint on the brush is visually consistent with the paint found on the statue.

The brush may have been discarded by the person responsible for the vandalism.

Investigation Significance

VERY HIGH

The partial fingerprint may potentially identify whoever handled the brush. The brush also provides evidence that the vandal was physically present in the courtyard.

Investigator's Note

Why leave the brush behind? Either the suspect panicked or they wanted us to find it. Fingerprint analysis should be prioritised.
