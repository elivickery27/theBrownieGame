Evidence #001

The Empty Recipe Folder

Status:Relevant

Location Found:Kitchen Office

Date Found:14 October

Time Found:4:18 PM

Description

A red recipe folder was discovered on the desk inside the school kitchen office at approximatley 4:18 PM. The folder usually contains the original brownie recipe along with several othe confidential recipes. However, upon inspection, the folder was found to be empty. The absence of the recipe raises concerns about its whereabouts.

Initial Observations

  • The folder showed no visible signs of damage.
  • The infamous recipe was missing from the folder.
  • No pages other than the brownie recipe were missing.
  • The folder was found on the kitchen office desk.
  • No obvious signs of forced entry or tampering were observed.

A small piece of blue thread was discovered underneath the folder. The thread was collected and stored seperatley for futher examination.

Connection to the Case

The missing recipe is a crucial piece of evidence in this investigation. The fact that the folder was left behind suggests that the person was solely interested in the recipe and not the folder as a whole.

The lack of damage also suggests that the perpetrator who removed the recipe did not need to forcefully access the folder, indicating that they may have had prior knowledge of its location and contents.

Investigation Significance

The discovery of the empty recipe folder is a significant development in the investigation. It indicates that the person responsible likley knew where the recipe was stored and taht they had enough access to remove a single page without attracting attention.

Investigator's Note

The recipe folder was left behind completely intact, with only the original brownie recipe missing. The lack of damage suggests that the person responsible knew exactly what they were looking for and had enough access to remove the recipe without raising suspicion. Check the other evidence for clues about who knew the recipe's location and had access to the office.
