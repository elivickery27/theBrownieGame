Case #001 - The Stolen Brownie Recipe

Home Suspects Evidence Timeline Solve the Case

Mark Davies

Age:38

Role:Catering Supplier

Background

Mark has deliverd ingredients and fresh produce to Wesbrook Academy for three years. He regularly visits the kitchn and knows which ingredients are used to make the school's brownies.

Connection to the Recipe

Mark knows that the brownies are popular because he is constantly delivering chocolate to the school, he also knows that their recipe and origin is a closley guarded secret.

Statement

"I was there to drop offf the chocolate delivery, I don't know anything about a recipe going missing."

Alibi

  • 3:48 PM - Arrived at the school.
  • 3:56 PM - Delivered the chocolate order.
  • 4:01 PM - Signed out of the schol.

Evidence Against Mark Davies

  • He knew about the school's famous brownies.
  • He had access to the kitchen.
  • He had a possibel financial motive.

Evidence in the Favour of Mark Davies

  • He left the schol at approximatley 4:01 PM.
  • The recipe wasn't discovered missing until 4:18 PM
