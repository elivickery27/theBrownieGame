Daniel Price

Age:29

Role:Assistan Cook

Background

Daniel has worked at Westbrook Academy for two years. He regularly helps prepare the school's famous brownies. He is known for his friendly demeanor and willingness to help out in the kitchen, but he has also been known to be a bit careless at times.

Connection to the Recipe

Daniel has used the recipe many times but has never been given permission to take the original recipe outside the kitchen. He has expressed interest in learning more about the recipe and has asked many questions about it.

Statement

"I was cleaing the preparation area and putting away ingredients. I knew Helen had the recipe so I didn't think anything of it. I was just doing my job and making sure everything was ready for the next day."

  • 4:15 PM - Left the preperation area.
